# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary. The full text at that link applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to trading.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and trading styles.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not just for yourself.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address, email, account data or trades, without their explicit permission.
- Pushing people to buy or sell specific securities, promoting paid signal groups, referral links or pump schemes, or promising easy profits.
- Asking other people for API keys, account access or money.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainers. Use the contact option on a maintainer's GitHub profile, or open a minimal public issue that only asks for a private channel, without describing the incident. Reports are handled confidentially, and the privacy and security of the reporter are respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code. Consequences follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact.
